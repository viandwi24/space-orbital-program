## What surprised me

- **A standing suit sleeps.** After about 2 s at rest it goes to surface rails, so the first `crew-grab` in the e2e was refused with "not in flight". `crew-grab` now wakes the suit and the laddered vessels within 30 m, and a latch keeps both awake.
- **Focus has to wait for the snapshot.** `crew-eva` answers before the flight's snapshot holds the suit, so `switchVessel` refused it as "gone". The loop then saw the ship still focused and ended the EVA HUD. `eva()` now waits for the suit to appear.
- **Tides in a Δv test.** A 2.5 min jetpack burn 150 km up measured 2.9 % below the rocket equation, because suit and pod drift apart in different gravity. At 2 000 km the error falls under 1 %.
- **Legacy test worlds are Sandbox.** The boarding test has to turn the assist off to see the hatch rule.
