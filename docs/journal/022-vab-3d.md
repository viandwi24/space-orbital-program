How it works in the screen, for the next reader:

- **Place**: click a catalogue card (or Enter on it). Every free node that fits the part gets a ring, the ghost shows where it goes. Click a ring or the ghost to attach, or `Tab`/`Enter`. The part stays armed so a stack can be built downwards by clicking; `Esc` stops. An empty design offers the platform.
- **Edit**: click a part to select it; Move (`M`), Detach (`D`, the subtree stays on the floor as a loose assembly that can be picked up again), Delete (`Del`), and a stage selector. Undo and redo are `Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Shift+Z`.
- **Stage**: engines and decouplers are auto-staged from decoupler depth (stage `maxDepth - d`); dragging a chip between stages (or `Alt+Up/Down` on it, or the selector) turns auto-staging off until "Auto-stage". Per stage: Δv, TWR, burn time, for sea level or vacuum.
- **Check**: no engine, no control source, no stages, parts in no stage, empty stage, TWR below 1 at liftoff, loose parts are warnings (Launch asks for a confirmation); an empty or invalid design is an error that disables Launch.
- **Camera**: drag to orbit, Shift+drag or middle button to pan, wheel to zoom, `F` to frame; arrows, `+`/`-` on the focused view. The camera stays inside the hall.

## What surprised me

- **A black half screen** in the first warnings capture and in the live view: the loose-assembly framing asked for a camera distance of 40 m, which put the camera outside the hall (the walls are one-sided planes, so from outside they vanish). `approachCamera` interpolated towards a goal that was never clamped; only user input was. Now both the goal and the rendered camera are clamped by `maxDistanceInRoom` (tested for 16 yaws).
- **Everything white** on the first run: the baked environment (a few thousand candela of point lights inside the hall, the lamps emissive at 7) was an order of magnitude too bright for a scene lit by one key light. Lights, environment intensity and bloom were tuned live through `VabView.debug` (a small handle object like `Renderer.debug`) and the numbers written back.
- The highlight shader had a latent NaN (`pow` of a tiny negative number when the dot product rounds past 1) that the bloom blur would spread into black blocks; fixed with `max`, although it turned out not to be the cause of the black screen above.
- `PCFSoftShadowMap` was removed in three 0.186 (the first run logged a warning that it falls back to PCF); the view now asks for `PCFShadowMap`.
- The built-in browser's pane was hidden several times while another session used it, which stalls `requestAnimationFrame` and image decoding; captures needed a screenshot call first to wake it. Unrelated to the code.
- The Δv and TWR the VAB shows come out identical to the HUD without any glue: `designStats` is a thin arrangement of `stageDeltaV`.

## Decisions made

- ADR 0029: editor state as an immutable value with snapshots for undo; loose assemblies as one-root designs; attach targets computed from the stack rule; auto-staging by decoupler depth; the view as a plain class in the renderer package; icons rendered by the same renderer.
- Trip estimate: 1 300 m/s loss allowance (the roughly 3.4 km/s journal 006 budgets for this body, minus the rotation bonus), compared with the ascent Δv (first stage at sea level, later stages in vacuum). It is a rule of thumb, labelled as an estimate in the UI; it says "enough" for the stock vessel, which reached orbit with 516 m/s to spare.
- Categories come from module ids, not from a field on the part definition (no SDK change); recorded as a candidate in plan 029.
- `looseX` slots the loose assemblies beside the platform (7.6 m, alternating sides) and the camera widens to keep them in view when the room allows.
