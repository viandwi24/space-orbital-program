# Effects 3: wreckage, plasma shape, blackout, trails, clusters, KTX2 (plan 040)

Checked 2026-10-17. What the ADRs 0077 to 0081 rest on, and what could not be confirmed.

| Fact | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Shock stand-off of a sphere in a hypersonic flow, as a fit to experiments: `delta / R = 0.143 exp(3.24 / M^2)` (gamma 1.4). Attributed to Billig (1967) in the paper that quotes it; it grows without bound towards Mach 1. The original paper was not read. | `standoffRatio` (Mach held at 1.5 or more), ADR 0078 | https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00953 (equation 5, 2026-10-17); a second correlation, Seiff (1962) `0.78 rho1/rho2`, is named by the search results at https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.02885 and was not used |
| Radio blackout happens when the plasma frequency of the shock layer reaches the link frequency, so it needs a critical electron density that depends on the link frequency; the electron density depends on body shape, speed and altitude; communication returns at lower altitude because the speed falls and collisions of electrons with neutrals increase. In one simulation study the RAM-C reflectometers' cutoff densities were 1.52e19, 1.25e18, 1.37e17 and 1.54e16 m^-3 for four frequencies; the RAM-C-II electron data cover 60 to 80 km at about 7.62 km/s. | The shape of the blackout window and the choice of the stagnation flux as its proxy, ADR 0079. The numeric altitude window of the real RAM-C blackout could not be retrieved, so the game's thresholds are set against the home body's own returns, not copied. | https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.6635 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.18163 (search results 2026-10-17); the DRDO viscous shock layer paper (publications.drdo.gov.in, article 1089) could not be fetched |
| Condensation trails form when the exhaust mixing line crosses the liquid saturation curve (Schmidt-Appleman criterion: ambient air colder than a critical temperature and moister than a critical relative humidity); persistence needs ice supersaturation. No numeric critical temperatures were retrieved. | The game models neither temperature nor humidity, so the trail density depends on pressure only, ADR 0080 | https://doaj.org/article/1008ca67621e41bdb3df6e6603af2e22 (Schumann 1996, search result 2026-10-17); https://acp.copernicus.org/articles/24/5009/2024/acp-24-5009-2024.pdf |
| `basis_transcoder.wasm` is 515 kB (245 kB gzipped) and `basis_transcoder.js` 56 kB (15 kB gzipped) in `three@0.186.1/examples/jsm/libs/basis/` | KTX2 pushed to draft 065: about 260 kB gzipped of bundle for no textured part | measured locally, 2026-10-17 |
| Peak stagnation flux of a ballistic return to the home body (radius 600 km, mu 3.53e12), from 70 km at 2.3 km/s, scale height 5.6 km, Sutton-Graves `1.74e-4 sqrt(rho / 0.625) v^3`, ballistic coefficient 500, 1 500 and 4 000 kg/m^2, flight path angle 3, 8 and 20 degrees: 1.2e5 to 1.9e5, 2.2e5 to 3.3e5, 3.6e5 to 5.5e5 W/m^2 at 14 to 30 km | `PLASMA_FLUX_START/FULL` and `BLACKOUT_FLUX_START/FULL`, ADRs 0078 and 0079 | computed in this plan with a 2-D integration at 0.05 s (a throwaway script, not kept) |
