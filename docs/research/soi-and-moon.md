# Spheres of influence, patched conics and the moon's parameters

Checked 2026-10-17 (plan 044).

- **Laplace SOI radius** `r_SOI = a (m / M)^(2/5)`, `a` the smaller body's semi-major axis around the larger, `m`, `M` the masses. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_of_influence_(astrodynamics) (checked 2026-10-17). The page also derives the switch rule: use the frame whose perturbation ratio is smaller; the SOI is where the ratios are equal. `@orbital/kepler` `soiRadius` already implements the formula (plan 002).
- **Patched conics** keep position and velocity continuous at the boundary: leaving a body, `v_parent = v_body + v_rel`; entering, `v_rel = v_parent - v_body`, and likewise for the position. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patched_conic_approximation (checked 2026-10-17; the page states the continuity, the arrival relation follows from it). This is what the sim implements: a translation of origin and velocity, same axes in every frame (units-and-frames.md).
- **Reference moon (game with the same planet scale)**, for a Δv budget that stock parts can fly: radius 200 000 m, standard gravitational parameter 6.5138398e10 m³/s², semi-major axis 12 000 000 m, SOI 2 429 559 m. https://wiki.kerbalspaceprogram.com/wiki/Mun and search summary https://www.sites.google.com/view/kspmath (checked 2026-10-17; the wiki page itself was behind a bot wall, values taken from the search extract). Landing from low lunar orbit costs on the order of 575 to 800 m/s in forum reports (not authoritative). Orbital's moon uses its own numbers (ADR 0052): radius 190 km, mu 5.776e10, a 11 800 km; the budget is computed in the journal from our engine data, not taken from that game.
- Time-warp stopping at SOI changes: a transition is the event a player must see, so warp returns to 1 (ADR 0051). Convention check: common practice in the genre; no external normative source.
