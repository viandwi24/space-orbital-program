# Re-entry heating: formulas and limits

Checked 2026-10-16 (plan 014, ADR 0023).

- **Stagnation-point convective heating (Sutton-Graves):** `q = k·√(ρ/r_n)·v³`, W/m², with `ρ` the freestream density (kg/m³), `r_n` the nose radius (m), `v` the speed relative to the air (m/s). Original: Sutton & Graves, NASA TR R-376 (1971), https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19720003329 (abstract page only; the report PDF was not read). The constant for air is quoted as 1.74e-4 in https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.11831 and 1.75e-4 in https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.5134 (both SI inputs, W/m² out); the often-cited 1.7415e-4 was not found in a source I could open. The sim uses **1.74e-4** (`SUTTON_GRAVES_K`). It is a stagnation-point value; the sim applies it to the whole windward area, which overestimates heating, and it is the one lever that makes the game "hotter" if wanted. Wikipedia's atmospheric-entry article (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_entry) names the Fay-Riddell equation as the more exact stagnation model and has no Sutton-Graves entry; not used.
- **Radiation:** grey body `P = ε·σ·A·T⁴`, `σ = 5.670374419e-8 W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴` (exact since the 2019 SI revision). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan%E2%80%93Boltzmann_law. Net exchange with surroundings at `T_env` is `ε·σ·A·(T⁴ − T_env⁴)`. The sim uses ε = 0.8 and `T_env` = 288.15 K everywhere (also in vacuum: a documented simplification).
- **Skin material:** aluminium melts at 933.47 K, density 2.699 g/cm³, specific heat 896.9 J/(kg·K). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aluminium. The sim's heated skin is 3 mm of aluminium: 2700·900·0.003 = 7 290 J/(m²·K) per unit surface area (`SKIN_HEAT_CAPACITY`). Only the skin is heated, not the whole part mass: a re-entry lasts minutes, heat does not soak through a tank.
- **Kerbal Space Program for scale (not copied):** the KSP wiki lists the 1.25 m heat shield at 3 300 K maximum temperature (https://wiki.kerbalspaceprogram.com/wiki/Small_Heat_Shield, via search result; the Mk1 pod page itself was blocked). A community guide for KSP 1.0 says Squad gave heat shields 3 400, pods designed for re-entry 2 400 and other pods 2 000 (https://forum.kerbalspaceprogram.com/topic/106241-guide-new-temperature-rules-for-parts-in-10-102-updates, search snippet only). KSP's heating is scaled much hotter than Sutton-Graves gives, so those limits do not transfer; ours are chosen against the sim's own heating (ADR 0023).
- **Measured in this sim** (plan 014 journal): a probe on a 2.9 degree deorbit path from 70 km at 2 250 m/s peaks at about 0.9-1.0 kK on its windward end; the same with a shield stays at the 700 K ablation temperature.
