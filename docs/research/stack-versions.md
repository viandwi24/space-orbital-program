- **WebGPU adapter info in Chrome on Apple silicon:** `vendor "apple"`, `architecture "metal-3"`, `device ""`, `description ""`: the chip model is not exposed, so a tier cannot tell an M2 from an M5 by name; the display's refresh rate is the signal (`device-tier.ts`).
- **three r186.1 `BundleGroup`** (plan 396, read 2026-10-24): `three/webgpu` exports it (`src/renderers/common/BundleGroup.js`); the group and its descendants are recorded as one WebGPU render bundle, `static` is true by default and `needsUpdate = true` re-records it; lights are not allowed inside; on a WebGL backend it draws as an ordinary group. **Long Animation Frames** (`PerformanceObserver` type `long-animation-frame`, Chrome 123+; entries over 50 ms with `blockingDuration` and `scripts[]` of `sourceURL`, `sourceFunctionName`, `invoker`, `duration`; https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/long-animation-frames).
- **three r186.1 timestamps** (`WebGPUTimestampQueryPool.resolveQueriesAsync`, read 2026-10-24): `timestamps` is a Map of uid `r:<frameCalls>:<renderContextId>:f<frame>` to ms for the contexts of the last resolved frame; the returned total is the last frame's sum, not the sum of all frames since the last read. Command encoders are labelled `renderContext_<id>`.

## TAAU and FSR 1 in three 0.186.1 (plan 393, 2026-10-24)

- `three/addons/tsl/display/TAAUNode.js` exports `taau(beauty, depth, velocity, camera)`. It sets the camera's view offset (a Halton sequence of 32 offsets, jitter shrunk to one output pixel) in `OnBeforeRenderPipeline` and clears it after, so anything that reads the camera's matrices at draw time sees the jitter and anything copied on the CPU before the pipeline renders does not. MSAA must be off. `currentFrameWeight` 0.025, `depthThreshold` 0.0005, `maxVelocityLength` 128 px. Depth is converted from logarithmic depth when the renderer has it (`TAAUtils.js`).
- The resolve target and the history are the canvas's size and are re-seeded (a bilinear upscale of the beauty) whenever the history target's size differs from the drawing buffer's: setting `_historyRenderTarget.setSize(1, 1)` forces it (a private field; `taau-cuts.ts` relies on it).
- The velocity texture is NDC current minus NDC previous (`velocity` in TSL, set up with `pass.setMRT(mrt({ output, velocity }))`); the node reads it at the dilated depth tap and uses `uv - velocity * (0.5, -0.5)`. A material with its own `mrtNode` is merged into the pass's; without a pass MRT its `mrtNode` replaces the colour output.
- `PassNode.setResolutionScale(s)` sizes the pass's target from the drawing buffer times `s`. `FSR1Node.js` (EASU + RCAS) exists in the same folder and is not used yet.


## Rapier height fields and CCD (plan 391, 2026-10-24)

- `@dimforge/rapier3d-compat` 0.21.0 (`dist/geometry/collider.d.ts`, `shape.d.ts`, `narrow_phase.d.ts`, read 2026-10-24). `ColliderDesc.heightfield(nrows, ncols, heights, scale, flags)`: `heights` is a column-major `(nrows + 1) × (ncols + 1)` matrix along local Y, columns along local X, rows along local Z, the grid centred on the origin and `scale.x` by `scale.z` wide (checked by dropping a box on a ridge). Tried for the ground and dropped (ADR 0337): **a height field's manifolds have `numContacts() > 0` and `numSolverContacts() == 0`** after a step, it has no thickness (a box that starts below it stays below), and with the surface 170 m from the body origin a resting pod rocked at 0.015 rad/s.
- `RigidBodyDesc.setCcdEnabled(true)` on dynamic bodies made a plane rolling at 70 m/s stick to the ground. A 0.1 m slab at 400 m/s from 0.5 m away stops on a fixed 0.1 m slab without it in this build.

## Discard and transient attachments in three 0.186.1 (plan 394, 2026-10-24)

- `WebGPUTextureUtils.js` adds `GPUTextureUsage.TRANSIENT_ATTACHMENT` itself when the constant exists and a multisampled colour or depth buffer is not stored (`renderTarget.storeMultisampledColorBuffer / storeMultisampledDepthBuffer === false`); `WebGPUBackend.js` then uses `storeOp: discard` and resolves in the pass.
