# 225 — EVA v2: suit focus, keys, jetpack, ladders, boarding at the hatch

## Goal

A kerbal-style EVA works from the keyboard. Stepping out moves focus and the HUD to the suit. The player walks, runs and jumps with WASD, Shift and Space, flies a 270 N jetpack with translation, grabs and climbs ladders with F, and boards with B at a hatch or ladder. A blocked hatch is refused with a reason.

## Scope

**In:**
- Focus moves to the suit on `crew-eva` and back on boarding, through the plan 221 switch. The HUD shows suit propellant, O₂, EC and radio.
- Keys: WASD walk (camera-relative), Shift run, Space jump, R jetpack on/off, WASDQE plus Shift/Ctrl translate while flying, F grab/release, B board.
- Jetpack: 270 N; propellant chosen so Δv lands in 300–600 m/s; translation on all axes with orientation assist toward the camera.
- `ladder` capability (a segment in part attach data). A ladder part, plus a short ladder at each crewed part's hatch.
- Grab within 1 m; climb up and down; the suit moves rigidly with the vessel while latched.
- `hatch` attach point on crewed parts. `crew-board` needs the suit within reach of a hatch or latched to a ladder on that vessel. Rule `stock.eva-board-assist` keeps the 50 m range, on in Sandbox only.
- Hatch obstruction probe on `crew-eva`, refused with "Hatch blocked by <part>".
- Boarding refills suit propellant from the vessel's own stock of the suit propellant (cold gas after plan 208) when the vessel carries it. A vessel without it refills nothing. No dependency on plan 211: monoprop refill is a follow-up if 211 lands first.
- Suit status chip shows "EVA" when out.

**Out:** ragdoll and fall damage, helmet, personal parachute, suit lights, EVA report, inventory, construction (P1/P2). Swimming.

## Design notes

- The suit stays a one-part vessel (ADR 0120). Focus switching is the same path as any vessel switch, so camera, HUD and scripts follow without special cases.
- Latched means the suit is parented to the vessel frame at a ladder parameter `s ∈ [0,1]`. Climb changes `s`. Release restores free motion with the vessel's velocity.

## Refinement (Worker D, 2026-10-19)

Checked against the code on `main` at f2b52448. Plans 203, 207, 208, 211, 215, 216 and 221 are done; the line numbers in Context are partly stale.

- **Focus.** There is no sim command for focus: `flight-loop.ts` `switchVessel` (plan 221) is reused. `eva()` switches to the suit; boarding switches back. Any focused vessel whose part declares `walk` is a suit, so `[`/`]` onto a suit shows the EVA HUD too.
- **Keys.** WASD, Shift, Space, R, F and B are all taken in the `flight` scope. EVA gets its own binding scope `eva`, the same in both profiles, active while a suit has focus, like `carry` (plan 217).
  - W/S/A/D walk camera-relative, Shift runs, Space jumps, R toggles the jetpack, F grabs or releases, B boards.
  - With the jetpack on: W/S/A/D translate camera-relative, Shift up, Ctrl down, Q/E turn the suit.
  - Latched: W/S climb, Space lets go.
- **Jetpack.** A module `stock.jetpack` replaces the suit's engine:
  - 270 N, Isp 200 s, 20 u of cold gas (1 u = 1 kg), so about 390 m/s with the suit's 90 kg;
  - it reads the existing `attitude.translate` (plan 211), so no new input field;
  - the web turns the camera-relative wish into the suit's frame;
  - suits already in old saves keep their engine module (module state is per part).
  - "Orientation assist" is Q/E turning plus the suit's reaction wheel.
- **Camera direction.** The renderer gets a small read-only `viewDirection()`, the only renderer change outside the ladder generator.
- **Ladders and hatches.** Optional part fields, mod API patch bump:
  - `ladder: { from, to, out }`, a segment in the part frame and the side a suit hangs on;
  - `hatch: { pos, dir }`.
  - The command pod gets a hatch and a short ladder. A radial `stock.ladder` part (2 m) is added.
- **New commands** (appended to the union):
  - `crew-grab { suit, release? }`: the nearest ladder within 1 m;
  - `crew-climb { suit, rate }`: −1..1 at 0.6 m/s;
  - `crew-jump { suit }`: 2.5 m/s up while standing.
  - Running is `walk.run` on the part (3 m/s).
- **Latch.** The suit's pose and velocity are set each step to the ladder point on the vessel (rigid), so the offset holds while the vessel turns.
- **Boarding.**
  - Within 2 m of a hatch's exit point, or latched to a ladder of that vessel.
  - Rule `stock.eva-board-assist` (on in Sandbox only) keeps the 50 m range.
  - The member keeps their suit's cold gas (`suitGas`, optional in the crew save). Boarding tops it up from the vessel's cold gas, and stepping out starts the suit with it (full the first time).
- **Hatch probe.** `crew-eva` checks a 1.2 m segment out of the seat part's hatch against the other parts' boxes, and refuses with "Hatch blocked by <part>". The suit appears 1 m out of the hatch. Parts without a hatch keep the old placement.
