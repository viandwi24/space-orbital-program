# 225 — EVA v2: suit focus, keys, jetpack, ladders, boarding at the hatch

## Goal

A kerbal-style EVA works from the keyboard. Stepping out moves focus and the HUD to the suit. The player walks, runs and jumps with WASD, Shift and Space, flies a 270 N jetpack with translation, grabs and climbs ladders with F, and boards with B at a hatch or ladder. A blocked hatch is refused with a reason.

## Scope

**In:**
- Focus moves to the suit on `crew-eva` and back on boarding, through the plan 221 switch. The HUD shows suit propellant, O₂, EC and radio.
- Keys: WASD walk (camera-relative), Shift run, Space jump, R jetpack on/off, WASDQE plus Shift/Ctrl translate while flying, F grab/release, B board.
- Jetpack: 270 N; propellant chosen so Δv lands in 300–600 m/s; translation on all axes with orientation assist toward the camera.
- `ladder` capability (a segment in part attach data). A ladder part, plus a short ladder at each crewed part's hatch.
- Grab within 1 m; climb up and down; the suit moves rigidly with the vessel while latched.
- `hatch` attach point on crewed parts. `crew-board` needs the suit within reach of a hatch or latched to a ladder on that vessel. Rule `stock.eva-board-assist` keeps the 50 m range, on in Sandbox only.
- Hatch obstruction probe on `crew-eva`, refused with "Hatch blocked by <part>".
- Boarding refills suit propellant from the vessel's own stock of the suit propellant (cold gas after plan 208) when the vessel carries it. A vessel without it refills nothing. No dependency on plan 211: monoprop refill is a follow-up if 211 lands first.
- Suit status chip shows "EVA" when out.

**Out:** ragdoll and fall damage, helmet, personal parachute, suit lights, EVA report, inventory, construction (P1/P2). Swimming.

## Design notes

- The suit stays a one-part vessel (ADR 0120). Focus switching is the same path as any vessel switch, so camera, HUD and scripts follow without special cases.
- Latched means the suit is parented to the vessel frame at a ladder parameter `s ∈ [0,1]`. Climb changes `s`. Release restores free motion with the vessel's velocity.

## Steps

1. Suit focus and the EVA HUD state.
2. Walk, run, jump keys wired to `crew-walk` and a jump impulse.
3. Jetpack 270 N with translation and orientation assist.
4. Ladder capability, ladder part, hatch rungs; grab, climb, release.
