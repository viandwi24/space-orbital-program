# 0273 — Star-system and mode art is generated from data; a mod may ship its own

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-22
**Plan:** plans/317-worlds-and-create-world-v3.md
**Builds on:** ADR 0184 (systems and scale variants), ADR 0094/0111 (rules and presets), ADR 0076 (mod assets); design `refs/space-orbital-program-ui-v3-2` (V3_CHANGES §3 and §4)

## Context

The v3-2 design draws pictures on the cards of Create World (a line-art illustration per mode, an orbit diagram per star system) and on the Worlds screen (a planet per world). Star systems and presets come from plugins: stock, Sol, and any mod that registers a system variant or a preset. The owner asked whether that art is custom per system, or something we make, and left the design of it to us.

Hand-drawn art per system cannot work. A mod's system would have no picture, or a wrong one, and every new stock or Sol variant would need an artist.

## Decision

1. **Generated by default.** `apps/web/src/lib/worlds/system-art.ts` builds a deterministic picture from a system's body definitions:
   - the star;
   - the planets in orbit order;
   - moons beside their planet;
   - sizes on a log scale of the radius;
   - a tint from what the data says: an atmosphere with hills is a blue world, craters without air are grey rock, a large body without a surface is a banded giant (outer ones turn blue);
   - small jitter from a seed of the system id.

   One function lays out both the card diagram and the square icon. `worldHue` gives each world's planet on the Worlds screen its colours, from the home body's tint and the world id. Mode cards use the same idea: an abstract orbit glyph from the preset id, in a colour from the id.
2. **A few palette hints.** The generator keeps a short table of tint and ring hints by body id. It covers Sol's planets, whose data does not say "red" for Mars or "rings" for Saturn. Nothing else is per system.
3. **The design's line art for the five stock modes.** Sandbox, Science, Career, Hardcore and Engineering are drawn as V3_CHANGES §4.2 shows, keyed by the stock preset id. Any other preset gets the generated glyph.
4. **Optional override from a mod.** `PresetDefinition` and `SystemVariantDefinition` get optional `illustration` (card art) and `icon` (square). Each is a `data:` URL of `image/svg+xml`, `image/png`, `image/webp` or `image/jpeg`, at most 128 KiB of text (`illustrationSchema` in core). The host draws them only as `<img>`, so SVG script never runs. When a mod gives one, it is used; otherwise the generated art. API 0.1.101 (additive).
5. **No image files per system in the repo**, and no new asset kind in mod packages (format 2 keeps `.glb` only). A `data:` URL is the simplest thing a mod author can paste.

## Alternatives considered

- **Art per system shipped with the game.** Rejected: mods would be second-class, and every variant needs drawing.
- **Image assets in mod packages.** Deferred: it needs a new asset type, hashing and a size budget in the package format. `data:` URLs cover the need now.
- **Rendering the real 3D planets into thumbnails.** Rejected for Create World: it needs a renderer and the planet bakes before the page can show a card.

## Consequences

- A gallery vitest renders every system the builtins load (stock, Sol 1:1, Sol rescaled) and every stock preset. It checks that the output is deterministic, finite and inside its box.
- A mod system looks like stock without any work, and a mod can still brand its own.
