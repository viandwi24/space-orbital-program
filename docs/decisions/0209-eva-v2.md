# 0209 — EVA v2

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Plan:** plans/225-eva-v2.md
**Builds on:** ADR 0120 (the suit is a vessel, the jetpack as an engine), ADR 0206 (key profiles), ADR 0208 (vessel switching), ADR 0203 (cold gas)

## Context

EVA works in the sim: step out, walk at up to 1.5 m/s, board within 50 m under 2 m/s with a free seat, plant a flag (`packages/sim/src/eva.ts:36-280`). It is driven only from the crew panel's buttons; there are no EVA keys (`flight-loop.ts:192-193,573-607`), and the suit's chip always reads "IVA" (`CrewPanel.svelte:75`). The jetpack is the stock engine on cold gas at 50 N and Isp 60 s, up only, about 31 m/s of Δv (`mods/stock/src/eva.ts:28-29,58-73`). There are no ladders and no climbing. KSP1 (verified, research doc 16): jetpack 270 N, 5 units of propellant (8 with a cylinder), about 600 m/s, lift-off only below 3.2 m/s² gravity. Rancangan §9.1–9.3.

## Decision

1. **Focus and HUD move to the suit** on EVA: a suit HUD shows propellant, O₂ and radio. The suit is the active vessel (ADR 0208), so `[`/`]` return to the ship.
2. **Keys** (both profiles, ADR 0206): WASD walk, Shift run, Space jump; R jetpack on/off; with the jetpack on, WASDQE and translation keys move and orientation assist holds the view; F grab a ladder or rung; B board; O helmet and P personal chute reserved for P1.
3. **Jetpack 270 N,** KSP's thrust, with Δv of 300–600 m/s set by the propellant load (default per owner review, rancangan §15 #12; may change). Full translation, not up only. KSP's 600 m/s is the upper reference.
4. **Ladders and rungs:** a ladder part and hatch rungs on pods. F grabs, W/S climb, Space lets go.
5. **Boarding only at a hatch or a ladder.** A blocked hatch says why ("Hatch blocked by part …"). The 50 m radius stays only in Sandbox as an assist (default per owner review, rancangan §15 #4; may change).

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep 50 N and up-only thrust** — rejected: a suit cannot leave the Moon, which is a KSP staple.
- **Keep 50 m boarding everywhere** — rejected: it removes the need to land near the craft.

## Consequences

- Plan 225 delivers it; plan 227 adds jetpack and EVA sounds; plan 231 places the ladder in the tech tree.
- The cold-gas resource (ADR 0203 point 2) is what the jetpack burns.
- The crew chip shows EVA while the member is outside.
